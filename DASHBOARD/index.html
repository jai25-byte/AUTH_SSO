Frequently Asked Questions

What is VaultAuthSSO?

VaultAuthSSO is your enterprise Identity and Access Management (IAM) provider, allowing you to access all company tools securely with a single login.

Why did I receive an OTP on sign-in?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is enforced to ensure that even if someone guesses your password, they cannot access your account without the OTP code sent to your email.

How do I revoke an app's access?

Go to the Connected Apps tab, locate the application, and click "Revoke". This will invalidate the integration tokens immediately.
